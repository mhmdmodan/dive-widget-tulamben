How to read this

Scores combine swell energy reaching the bed, current, runoff and wind, each weighted by what that particular site is sensitive to. Open any lever in the site panel to see its full derivation.

Tide heads the page rather than each site: sea level differs by under 0.03 m across the whole coast. Slack water for current does not line up with high or low tide here — the flow is driven by the Lombok Strait throughflow, which runs out of phase with local sea level.

This is a planning aid, not a safety forecast. Visibility is inferred, never observed. The marine model's nearest wet cell is 15–21 km offshore, so several sites share one forecast and differ only by calibration. Internal waves and the sudden cold down-currents at the Drop-Off are invisible to every public model. Confirm with a local operator before you get in.
